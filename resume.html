Profile

Final-year Mechanical and Mechatronic Engineering (Honours) student with three years of full-cycle engineering delivery on the UTS Motorsports Formula SAE team, progressing from team member to Chassis Lead and then Mechanical Lead, and currently working as a data and race engineer in GT3 competition. I take systems from concept through CAD, analysis and design for manufacture, then fabricate, assemble and test them myself, and I am accountable for whether they work. As Mechanical Lead in 2025 I directed around twenty engineers across six subsystems and delivered the first UTS car in years to pass all technical scrutineering and complete every dynamic event. Alongside the mechanical work I design and build the electronics, data and software systems around it, from PCBs and embedded firmware to race telemetry and team infrastructure. Seeking a graduate engineering role where design ownership stays connected to real hardware.

Technical skills

Design and CAD: SolidWorks, design for manufacture, tolerancing and tolerance stack-up, design to a defined rule set (FSAE technical regulations), full-vehicle packaging and systems integration.

Analysis and testing: Ansys FEA, kinematic and thermal calculation, destructive and non-destructive mechanical testing, experimental verification of predicted performance, measurement and tolerance characterisation of tooling.

Manufacturing: TIG welding in steel, stainless and aluminium; manual mill and lathe; CNC milling and CNC plasma tube notching; waterjet and laser cutting; 3D printing in ABS and nylon; carbon and fibreglass composite layup.

Electronics and PCB: Altium schematic capture and layout, analogue signal conditioning and filter design, sensor integration, STM32 and Arduino microcontrollers, ESP32, FPGA work in Intel Quartus Prime, STM32CubeIDE.

Software and control: Python and PyQt6, C and C++, ROS, MATLAB, embedded firmware, control systems and custom operator interfaces, Linux server administration, WireGuard networking.

Data and specialist systems: Race telemetry and data acquisition, CAN bus, Racelogic VBOX, MoTeC i2 Pro and T2 Server, Bosch RaceCon and WinDarab, 600 V tractive systems and high voltage safety practice.
